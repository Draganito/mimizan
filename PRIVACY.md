# Privacy

Mimizan Lab does not collect, store, or transmit personal data.

Files you open stay on your computer. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising, and it does not connect to a network.

The build from the Mac App Store offers one purchase. Apple's App Store carries it out under your Apple account; the app asks the App Store whether the purchase is owned and learns nothing else.
